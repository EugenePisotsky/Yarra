# Experiment ledger

Decisions and observations through 2026-09-21. Current behavior belongs in [architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md); reproduction and measurement rules belong in [performance](PERFORMANCE.md). This ledger replaces the individual experiment reports. **Retained means present/useful, not necessarily a proven frame-time or energy improvement.** Rejected trials should not be revived without a new hypothesis.

Most desktop measurements used M2 Max; early phone work used iPhone 15 Pro Max / A17 Pro. Scenes, pixels, density, clocks and measurement methods changed. Compare only matched runs within an entry. A counter reduction, Xcode replay duration, sampled GPU span, Metal HUD duration and delivered frame interval are different measurements. Temporal GPU markers are known to undercount. No result here establishes mainstream-PC 1440p/60 acceptance or sustained phone 60 FPS.

**Density correction:** September 15–16 default-fixture captures labelled “44 roots/m²” actually used **66**. The cooked catalog is authoritative. Historical 32 m grass fixtures are different from the current 8 m authored world. Later 96/128-density fixtures are explicit variants. Old report labels inside raw archives remain historical; use this correction when interpreting them.

## Tree LOD material publication — September 23

**Retained correctness fix.** The native offscreen forest-tree test, using the debug test build on macOS/Metal with Temporal disabled, reproduced missing foliage on the first frame of all eight switches through four imported Tree 07 LODs; bark remained drawable. Wind material conversion ran after `AssetEventSystems` because it waited for calculated bounds. Cloud/wind material conversion now finishes before asset events and render specialization; bounds expand separately before culling. The same eight transitions now have no material/color-pipeline gaps, including the final test's depth/motion-prepass readiness checks. [Before](performance/20260923-tree-lod/before.log), [after](performance/20260923-tree-lod/after.log), [regression](../crates/engine/src/tree_wind/scene_tests.rs). This isolates the canopy-dropout defect; the preceding whole-view Temporal roughness and reported frame-time spikes are not proven to share that cause. Every history/target reset can now be logged with `--metalfx-timing-log`; no AA quality, LOD thresholds or reset policy changed. No performance gain claimed.

## Grass residency and temporal history — September 23

**Retained correctness fix; user confirmed the gameplay flicker is resolved.** After the tree-material fix, the user isolated the continuing whole-view flicker to grass: it persisted with terrain/objects/clouds disabled and stopped with grass disabled. A native offscreen MetalFX test on M2 Max (optimized debug test build, 128×128 input / 256×256 output) then reproduced 24 whole-view history resets in 24 offscreen page additions/removals. Resident-page repacking was treated as a global history cut. Catalog revisions are now tracked separately: residency and grass visibility changes retain history, while catalog edits, explicit camera cuts and origin/time discontinuities retain invalidation. Current stable roots/seeds rebuild previous blade poses when packed indices change. Queueing also no longer uses the previous frame's active flag, which caused a missing first grass draw after an empty scene or re-enable.

The same 24 updates now produce **zero history resets**, across prepared and procedural geometry, stationary cameras, strafing and wind. Empty/populated and disabled/enabled transitions retain history and draw immediately; camera-only motion agrees with depth reprojection to p95 <0.001 input pixel. Explicit camera cuts and catalog edits still reset. [Before](performance/20260923-grass-temporal-streaming/before.log), [after](performance/20260923-grass-temporal-streaming/after.log), [regression](../crates/vegetation_render/src/renderer/blade_preparation/temporal_streaming_tests.rs). No whole-game performance gain measured: native reset sets a scaler flag rather than reallocating it; page uploads/cache rebuilding and previous-pose work remain. Earlier Temporal cost/strafe-blur findings remain open. `--metalfx-timing-log` now additionally records grass source changes and reset reasons as `GRASS_TEMPORAL_HISTORY`.

| Change | Decision / observation |
| --- | --- |
| Weather visibility through Bevy's aerosol medium | **Rejected.** Storm (1.6 km) and faintly Rain (4 km) produced coloured one-pixel horizontal streaks that persisted with Linear upscaling and with clouds off. Bevy's `sample_transmittance_lut_segment` divides two half-float transmittances that underflow for ground-hitting rays through dense haze; 0/0 becomes NaN, which bloom spreads. Haze in that model is also sun-lit under a full cloud deck, which washed out overcast scenes. | Weather fog in the cloud composite | **Retained.** Adds only the extinction missing from the authored haze; clear weather keeps the previous early exit below the horizon. Averaged transmittance over resolved MSAA colour left bright halos where silhouettes met the horizon sky; edges touching the sky now take the sky's transmittance (at most one pixel of lost edge smoothing, no effect without MSAA). Cost unmeasured. |
| Sky cache invalidation | **Changed.** Continuous weather values no longer invalidate the cache (previously a full 512² trace every frame of a transition). Refresh is now a per-frame row budget instead of quarter-atlas stripes every ~31 ms; same eight complete refreshes per second. Unit-tested, not timed. |
| Wind during weather changes | **Retained.** `VegetationWind::rate` scales the phase clock (clamped so one frame stays below the grass history-reset threshold); changing `speed` would jump every wave. |
| Close-up lighting under cloud (user report, Temporal) | **Changed.** Near tree trunks looked fogged next to dark grass; fog is ~0.4% at 3–5 m, so it was lighting. Bevy's ambient term is a large share of clear-day light, and −1.5…−2.5 EV offsets made ambient-lit PBR surfaces brighter than sunlit ones, while grass bounds ambient at 0.60 exposed and stayed dark. Blue clear-sky ambient on pale bark matched the fog tint. Now: offsets −0.8/−1.0/−0.9 EV, weather greys skylight colour, grass ambient bound scales with exposure adaptation only (a linear on-screen ambient gain made grass brighter than sunlit grass), and sun shadows no longer darken grass ambient where clouds block the sun. Eye-checked only. |
| Cloud sky "lines" (user report, predates weather) | **Changed.** Distant clouds showed a regular diagonal sawtooth. Cause: the 512² stereographic cache maps the horizon to a circle, so 1–2-texel cloud slivers crossed the square texel grid diagonally and bilinear filtering showed the grid. Per-texel ray jitter with refresh accumulation was tried first and **rejected**: no visible change, so ray-march slicing was not the cause. Replaced by a 2048×256 horizon-aligned panorama at 4 Hz (same rays per second as 512² at 8 Hz; ~5.7 vs ~4.2 texels per degree of azimuth, about double the elevation resolution near the horizon). An intermediate 1024×256 removed the staircases but blurred clouds horizontally. Azimuth seam checked by rotating it into view. [Before/after](performance/20260925-weather/sky-cache-before-after.jpg). Post-hitch refresh bound kept at 65,536 rays. Eye-checked, not timed. |
| Visible cloud "morphing" during weather changes | **Changed.** Two causes: every texel updated only four times per second with a refresh cursor sweeping the sky, and one global coverage threshold grew or shrank all clouds at once. The cache now keeps three images and the composite cross-fades the two latest complete refreshes (+8 MiB, one extra texture sample per sky pixel, unchanged tracing); cloud shape blends region by region from a noise arrival field already sampled by the density function. F1 blends default to 60 s again. A temporary Clear→Storm 10 s capture showed patches forming before the deck closes. Smoothness in motion and cost are not measured. |
| F1 preset clicks "did nothing" | **Changed.** Handler verified by test; the 60 s default blend was the cause. Default became 10 s, then returned to 60 s once clouds changed region by region. |
| Rain, first version | **Retained for tuning.** Instanced streaks in near/mid/far boxes (3,000/6,000/8,000 drops at Storm), drawn after Temporal reconstruction or on the resolved MSAA image; soft depth occlusion; wetness darkens PBR surfaces and grass. Native snapshots of Rain (Spatial, 4× MSAA) and Storm (Temporal) plus an accelerated wet/dry comparison. Density, brightness and wet-ground sheen are first guesses; no shelter under trees, splashes or puddles yet. Cost unmeasured; F1 **Rain rendering** isolates it. |

## Open gates and maintenance

The remaining gates are sustained terrain/whole-game power, Temporal cost and motion quality, field-scale grass lighting, target-PC acceptance, and physical-phone heat/60-FPS delivery. Keep correctness references until their replacements pass the relevant gate. Existing counters often identify less work without demonstrating better delivered frames or lower power.

For each new experiment update one row or add a compact entry: date/ID, hypothesis, retained/rejected/unresolved decision, device/build and workload, before/after metric, limitation, evidence link. Do not create another standalone report. Raw evidence lives under `performance/`; prose removed during consolidation remains in Git at `2afce93`. New architecture proposals and cleanup work belong in [refactoring](REFACTORING.md), not this results ledger.
